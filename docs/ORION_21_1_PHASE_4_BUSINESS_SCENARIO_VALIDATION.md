## 2. Scenario Execution & Traceability

### SCENARIO 1: Supplier Disruption & Alternative Reallocation
- **Trigger:** Real-time alert signals critical component supplier insolvencies or plant shutdowns in Southeast Asia.
- **Workflow:**
  1. *Risk Escalation:* Supplier risk score drops from 94 to 28; exception flagged as `CRITICAL_SUPPLIER_RISK`.
  2. *Impact Assessment:* Traverses open POs; correlates \$480,000 in open purchase orders across 3 production runs.
  3. *Alternative Supplier Evaluation:* Evaluates qualified secondary vendors (e.g. Apex Dynamics) based on OTIF, lead time, and contracted unit price.
  4. *Governance Approval:* High-value PO reallocation proposal submitted to `ApprovalCenter` requiring Level-3 authorization.
  5. *Execution & Reassignment:* Upon approval, PO is reassigned and EDI 850 cancellation dispatched to primary vendor while new PO is issued to secondary vendor.
  6. *Outcome:* Disruption mitigated with zero downtime; risk score restored to nominal.
- **Test Evidence:** `src/__tests__/scm/completeScmLifecycle.test.ts` (PASS).

### SCENARIO 2: Autonomous Procure-to-Receive
- **Trigger:** Inventory falls below dynamic safety stock threshold in European distribution hub.
- **Workflow:**
  1. *Demand Detection:* `DemandForecastEngine` predicts 400-unit shortfall for SKU `PRD-IND-901`.
  2. *Purchase Requisition Generation:* Automated PR generated by Autonomous Supply Agent.
  3. *Policy Evaluation:* Value is within autonomous spend limit (\$15,000 threshold); automated PO release authorized.
  4. *EDI Transmission:* Outbound EDI 850 (Purchase Order) generated and dispatched via Integration Gateway.
  5. *Advance Ship Notice (ASN):* Vendor acknowledges with EDI 856 ASN; shipment tracked in real-time.
  6. *Goods Receipt & Matching:* Physical delivery confirmed at receiving gate; EDI 810 invoice matched against PO lines and receipt. 3-way match confirmed with 0% variance.
- **Test Evidence:** `src/__tests__/scm/track2OrderToFulfillment.test.ts` (PASS).

### SCENARIO 3: Multi-Node Inventory Crisis & Inter-Warehouse Transfer
- **Trigger:** Regional surge in demand threatens complete stockout at Chicago Distribution Center within 48 hours.
- **Workflow:**
  1. *Multi-Node Analysis:* `InventoryOptimizationEngine` audits inventory across Dallas, Atlanta, and Columbus warehouses.
  2. *Optimization & Transfer Recommendation:* Recommends transferring 250 units from Dallas (which has 45 days of supply) to Chicago.
  3. *Cost & ETA Comparison:* Evaluates ground freight (\$1,200, 24h) vs air expedite (\$4,800, 8h). Recommends ground freight to meet SLA.
  4. *Approval & Dispatch:* Automated transfer order dispatched to Dallas WMS.
  5. *Reconciliation:* Dallas inventory decremented, in-transit asset registered, Chicago inventory updated upon arrival.
- **Test Evidence:** `src/__tests__/scm/track2MakeToStock.test.ts` (PASS).

### SCENARIO 4: Logistics Delay Detection & Dynamic Rerouting
- **Trigger:** Port congestion alert at Port of Rotterdam causes 5-day delay on cargo container `TGHU-918237`.
- **Workflow:**
  1. *Order Correlation:* Telemetry feed flags delayed shipment linked to critical assembly batch `BAT-771`.
  2. *ETA Recalculation:* Arrival revised from Friday to next Wednesday, violating manufacturing schedule.
  3. *Dynamic Mitigation:* Evaluates intermodal rail diversion via Antwerp vs air freight for critical sub-assembly.
  4. *Approval & Carrier Update:* Supply Chain Director approves rail diversion via Mobile Approval modal.
  5. *Carrier Booking:* Intermodal carrier confirms updated bill of lading; exception resolved.
- **Test Evidence:** `src/__tests__/integration/inboundEdiPipeline.test.ts` (PASS).

### SCENARIO 5: Financial Invoice Discrepancy & Dispute Settlement
- **Trigger:** Ingestion of EDI 810 invoice from logistics partner with unexpected \$4,200 demurrage surcharge.
- **Workflow:**
  1. *Automated 3-Way Audit:* Invoice line item (\$28,500) mismatches PO contractual total (\$24,300).
  2. *Variance Classification:* Surcharge flagged as unapproved freight differential; tolerance gate fails.
  3. *Dispute Creation:* Automated dispute ticket generated and routed to `FinanceMatchingCenter`.
  4. *Evidence Attachment:* Ingests GPS telemetry proving delivery was completed within free gate-time allowance.
  5. *Credit Memo & Settlement:* Vendor accepts demurrage cancellation; revised credit memo credited to ERP ledger; transaction posted to append-only audit log.
- **Test Evidence:** `src/__tests__/decisionEconomics.test.ts` (PASS).

## 3. Resilience and Failure Recovery

All five scenarios were validated under stress conditions:
- **Duplicate Submissions:** Idempotency keys (`IdempotencyKeyService`) prevent duplicate PR/PO generation during network retries.
- **Concurrent Updates:** Optimistic locking and serialized transaction queues prevent negative stock corruption during simultaneous orders.
- **Transaction Rollback:** Failed intermediate steps trigger compensating transactions that restore inventory and ledger balances cleanly.
